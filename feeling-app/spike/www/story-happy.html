Sprocket's Sunny Saturday

a story about the happy feeling

Saturday morning. Sunlight came through the window, and Sprocket's favorite breakfast was waiting on the table.

Sprocket's steps felt light and bouncy. Their shoulders felt loose, and a smile came easy, without even trying.

Sprocket's thoughts were easy and warm: Today feels good. No big reason needed.

Sprocket noticed: my steps feel light, my smile feels easy. This feeling has a name — it's called HAPPY.

Happy visits on ordinary good days too, not just birthdays or big news. You don't need a big reason to feel it.

So Sprocket tried something new: savoring. Pause for a moment, and really notice one good thing happening right now.

Sprocket paused and really felt the warm sun on their back, and listened to their favorite song playing softly. Just that, for a moment.

So next time your steps feel light and your smile feels easy, try what Sprocket tried. Notice it. Name it. Try a tool — pausing to savor a good moment counts.
