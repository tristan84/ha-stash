Sprocket's Big Bike Ride

a story about the proud feeling

Tap the arrow to begin →

Sprocket had practiced and practiced. Today was the day to try riding without training wheels — wobbly, wiggly, but ready.

Pedal, wobble, wobble — and then, balance! Sprocket's chest felt warm and glowing, and they sat up tall on the seat.

Sprocket's thoughts were bright and loud: I did it! I actually did it!

Sprocket stopped and noticed: my chest feels warm, I'm standing tall. This feeling has a name — it's called PROUD.

Proud visits after trying something hard and sticking with it — big or small, wobbly or perfect, it all counts.

So Sprocket tried something new: strike a proud pose — arms up high! — and then find someone to share it with.

Sprocket rode right up to their grown-up friend: "I rode my bike, look!" Sharing the moment made it even better.

So next time your chest feels warm and you're standing tall, try what Sprocket tried. Notice it. Name it. Try a tool — a proud pose and sharing it both count.
