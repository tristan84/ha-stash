Sprocket's Very Full Day

a story about the overwhelmed feeling

Tap the arrow to begin →

It was a big birthday party — balloons popping, music playing, friends calling Sprocket's name from every direction, all at once.

Everything started to feel tight and heavy. Sprocket's head felt loud and foggy, and they wanted to cover their ears.

Sprocket's thoughts scattered every which way: Too much! Too much! I don't know where to start.

Sprocket stopped and noticed: my body feels tight, my head feels loud. This feeling has a name — it's called OVERWHELMED.

Overwhelmed visits when there's too much happening at once for your brain and body to sort through. It doesn't mean anything's wrong with you.

So Sprocket tried something new: step away to a quiet corner for a moment. Then, pick just ONE small thing to do next.

In the quiet, Sprocket chose one small thing: find just one friend to sit with. The rest of the party could wait its turn.

So next time everything feels like too much, try what Sprocket tried. Notice it. Name it. Try a tool — a quiet moment and picking one small thing both count.
