Sprocket's Wobbly Tower

a story about the frustrated feeling

Tap the arrow to begin →

Sprocket was building the tallest tower ever — five blocks, six blocks, seven!

Then — CRASH — the tower tumbled down. Sprocket's cheeks went hot, and both hands squeezed into tight little fists.

Sprocket's thoughts got loud: This is impossible. I can't do this. I want to give up!

Sprocket stopped and noticed: my face feels hot, my hands feel tight. This feeling has a name — it's called FRUSTRATED.

Frustrated visits everyone when something is hard, or doesn't go the way we planned. It doesn't mean you're bad at it.

So Sprocket tried something new: shake out your hands, and count to five. One… two… three… four… five.

The tower was still a little wobbly. The frustrated feeling was still a little there. But Sprocket picked up a block and tried again — and felt proud. Not for a perfect tower. For trying again.

So next time your face feels hot or your hands feel tight, try what Sprocket tried. Notice it. Name it. Try a tool — even asking for help counts.
