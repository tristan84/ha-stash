Sprocket's Spilled Juice

a story about the embarrassed feeling

It was lunchtime. Sprocket reached across the table for a napkin — and their elbow bumped right into the juice cup.

The cup tipped — splash! — right in front of everyone. Sprocket's cheeks went hot, their tummy dropped, and they wanted to shrink down small.

Sprocket's thoughts replayed it again and again: Everyone's looking at me. I'm so silly.

Sprocket stopped and noticed: my cheeks feel hot, I want to hide. This feeling has a name — it's called EMBARRASSED.

Embarrassed visits after a mix-up happens in front of others. Everyone spills sometimes — even grown-ups. It doesn't erase all the good things about you.

So Sprocket tried something new: a little shake — arms, shoulders, wiggle it out — and remember, one spill doesn't undo everything good about today.

Sprocket shook it off, grabbed a towel, and helped clean up — even managing a small smile.

So next time your cheeks feel hot and you want to hide, try what Sprocket tried. Notice it. Name it. Try a tool — a shake-off and remembering everyone messes up both count.
