Sprocket's Bouncy Morning

a story about the excited feeling

Tap the arrow to begin →

Today, Sprocket's best friend was coming to visit! Sprocket had thought about it all week.

Sprocket's legs started bouncing all on their own — boing, boing, boing! And Sprocket's heart went pitter-patter-pitter-patter fast.

Sprocket's thoughts bounced too — what games will we play, what will we build, will there be snacks? Thoughts everywhere, all at once!

Sprocket stopped bouncing just long enough to notice: my legs are bouncy, my heart is fast, my thoughts are racing. This feeling has a name — it's called EXCITED.

Excited can feel a lot like worried in the body — fast heart, wiggly legs. But it comes with a happy feeling too. Both are big feelings. Both are okay.

So Sprocket tried something fun: a big wiggle dance, to let the bounces out. Shake, shake, wiggle, wiggle!

After wiggling it out, Sprocket felt ready — still excited, just settled excited. And proud, for noticing the big feeling and giving it somewhere to go.

So next time your legs feel bouncy or your thoughts race with excitement, try what Sprocket tried. Notice it. Name it. Try a tool — even a wiggle dance counts.
