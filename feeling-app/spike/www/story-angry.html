Sprocket's Scattered Puzzle

a story about the angry feeling

Tap the arrow to begin →

Sprocket was SO close to finishing the big puzzle — just three pieces left!

Then a big gust of wind swept through and scattered every piece — whoosh! Sprocket's face went hot all at once, and both hands squeezed into tight fists.

Sprocket's thoughts got loud and fast: That's not fair! I want to yell!

Sprocket stopped and noticed: my face feels hot, my hands feel tight, fast. This feeling has a name — it's called ANGRY. It's bigger and faster than frustrated.

Angry visits when something feels unfair, or gets ruined or taken away. It's a big feeling — and big feelings sometimes need a big, safe release.

So Sprocket tried something new: stomp your feet like a big dinosaur — stomp, stomp, stomp — then tell a grown-up what happened.

After stomping it out, Sprocket walked over and told their grown-up friend exactly what happened. Saying it out loud helped too.

So next time your face feels hot and fast, try what Sprocket tried. Notice it. Name it. Try a tool — a big stomp and asking for help both count.
